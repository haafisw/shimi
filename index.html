FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's free, and what needs Pro?

Enhance 2× and 4×, scene looks, Wallpaper, Remove BG and Eraser are free. On iPhone, Pro adds 8× and 12× upscaling, batches of up to 20 photos and video tools.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Enhancing, background removal, erasing and video all run on your iPhone or Mac.

Will Shimi change my colours?

Clean-up and upscaling keep your colours. Looks like Colour match only apply when you turn them on.

How big can results get?

You can choose 2×, 4×, 8× or 12×. On iPhone, results stop at 8192 pixels on the long side to stay within the phone's memory.

Which file types does it open?

JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WEBP, GIF and RAW photos, plus MP4 and MOV video.

Does a video keep its format?

Yes. An .mp4 stays an .mp4, and the original sound is kept.

Can Shimi set my iPhone wallpaper directly?

iOS doesn't let apps do that. Shimi saves the finished wallpaper to Photos, and you set it from there with Share ▸ Use as Wallpaper.
