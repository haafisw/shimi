Privacy Policy

Your pictures are yours.

Effective November 21, 2026

The short version

Shimi works on your iPhone or Mac. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share your photos, videos or personal data. Shimi has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Shimi apps for iPhone and Mac and this website. “We” means the developer of Shimi.

Your photos and videos

Enhancing, background removal, erasing, wallpapers and video tools all run on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Your pictures and videos are not uploaded to us or to any server we run.

  • Choosing pictures: on iPhone, Shimi uses Apple's photo picker, so it only receives the items you choose. It does not read the rest of your library.
  • Saving results: if you save to Photos, iOS asks for add-only permission. This lets Shimi add new items without viewing your library.

What stays on your device

Shimi keeps a small amount of information in its own app storage so it can work:

  • The profile name, short bio and picture you choose to add
  • Small thumbnails of your recent creations
  • Your settings, such as appearance and save format

We cannot access this information. You can clear recent thumbnails in Settings, and deleting Shimi removes its app storage from your device. Your device's own backups may keep a copy until they are replaced, under your Apple Account settings.

Purchases

Shimi Pro subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Shimi checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your account. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information from Apple

Apple may give developers sales reports and summarized App Store statistics. If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers, or you test Shimi through TestFlight, Apple may also provide crash reports and related device information. We use these only to run and improve Shimi.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or advertising. It is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads one scrolling library from unpkg. Like most web services, those providers may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the page. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Shimi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information or private photos.
